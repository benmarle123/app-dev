# White Chicks

## About the Movie

**White Chicks** is a *comedy movie* from 2004. It stars Shawn and Marlon Wayans as two FBI agents.

> Two agents, one big disguise, and a lot of trouble.

---

### The Story

1. Two FBI agents fail a big case.
2. They get a new job to protect two hotel heiresses.
3. They go undercover as the heiresses to catch the bad guys.

### Why I Like It

- It is very funny.
- The disguises are over the top.
- I can watch it again and again.

### Movie Info

- Genre: `comedy`
- Year: `2004`
